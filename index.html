Key Capabilities

Designed for Institutional Excellence

A high-performance employee self-service platform connected directly to enterprise SAP HANA backends.

📊

Executive Dashboard

At-a-glance view of daily attendance, active leave balances, action-required managerial approvals, and quick administrative shortcuts.

✈️

Leave Administration

Submit annual, emergency, sick, or hajj leaves with file attachments, replacement colleague selection, and automated resumption filings upon return.

⚡

Managerial Approvals Hub

Line managers review, approve, or reject employee submissions on the go with complete audit trails, remark notes, and historical logs.

⏱️

Attendance & Biometrics

Inspect real-time check-in and check-out logs, monitor daily working hours, and request regularization with supporting justifications.

💵

Payroll & Payslips

Access transparent breakdowns of basic salary, allowances (housing, transport), PASI pension deductions, and download signed monthly PDF payslips.

🌐

Native Bilingual (AR / EN)

Seamless instant switching between Arabic (عربي) and English with full RTL (Right-to-Left) typography optimization for the Gulf region.

Institutional Trust

Enterprise Security Standards

Architected with strict data governance policies compliant with GCC institutional mandates.

🔐 Hardware Keystore & Keychain

Authentication tokens and sensitive credentials are encrypted using Apple Keychain services via hardware-backed security modules.

🛡️ End-to-End TLS / HTTPS

All client-server communications are conducted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols directly to the authorized GCC-STAT API gateway.

🏢 SAP HANA Enterprise Backbone

Direct transactional connectivity with SAP Business One and SAP HANA guarantees real-time record reconciliation and zero data discrepancy.

👥 Role-Based Access Controls

Strict access boundaries ensure staff only interact with their authorized records, with elevated workflows for designated supervisors.
