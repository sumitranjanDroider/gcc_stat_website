Self-Service Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick solutions for common questions regarding the GCC Stat mobile app.

The GCC Stat application is an enterprise internal application. In accordance with GCC-STAT security policies, self-registration is disabled. Your account credentials (Employee ID and initial secure password) are created and assigned directly by the GCC-STAT Human Resources Department upon appointment.

To reset your password, contact the GCC-STAT IT Help Desk at support@gccstat.org or submit a ticket using the form above. After identity verification with your official employee email and staff ID, a temporary password reset token will be dispatched to your registered institutional email.

Leave applications follow a multi-tier approval workflow (Replacement Colleague confirmation → Direct Line Manager → Department Director → HR Finalization). You can check the real-time stage of your application under Leave History → Stage Status. If a request is urgent, you can remind your supervisor directly.

Navigate to the Payroll module from the home dashboard or bottom navigation. Select the desired fiscal year and month, then tap on the Download Payslip (PDF) button. The document will open in the native iOS document previewer and can be saved or printed securely.

Biometric check-in and check-out logs are synchronized from physical office biometric turnstiles to the central SAP HANA database in automated 15-minute intervals. If your attendance does not show up after 30 minutes, submit an Attendance Regularization Request directly through the app.

The GCC Stat app is compatible with iPhone and iPad devices running iOS 14.0 or later. We recommend keeping your device updated to the latest iOS version for optimal performance, security patches, and biometric Face ID / Touch ID convenience.
