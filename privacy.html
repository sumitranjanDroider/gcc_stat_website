Privacy Policy for GCC Stat

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Organization: Statistical Centre for the Cooperation Council for the Arab Countries of the Gulf (GCC-STAT)  |  Application Identifier: com.tenxsf.gccstat

The Statistical Centre for the Cooperation Council for the Arab Countries of the Gulf ("GCC-STAT", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data protection, privacy, and confidentiality. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection of information obtained through the GCC Stat Employee Self-Service (ESS) mobile application (the "Application") distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

1. Scope and Target Audience

The Application is an enterprise workplace portal intended solely for authorized employees, contractors, and executive management of GCC-STAT. It is not intended for the general public, and account self-registration is strictly unavailable.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver employee self-service capabilities and fulfill statutory human resources obligations, the Application accesses and processes the following categories of data:

  • Professional Identity: Full name, employee identification number, job title, institutional email address, department, reporting manager, and contact numbers.
  • Leave & Workflow Records: Leave requests, quota balances, medical certificates, replacement employee designations, and managerial audit remarks.
  • Time & Attendance: Check-in/check-out timestamps synchronized from institutional biometric sensors, attendance regularization requests, and official mission justifications.
  • Financial & Payroll Data: Basic salary details, housing and transportation allowances, social insurance contributions (e.g., PASI), and encrypted monthly PDF payslips.
  • User-Submitted Documents: Photos and document attachments (PDFs, images) voluntarily uploaded by the user to support claims, leave requests, or administrative forms.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage (Android & iOS)

The Application requests access only to specific device features strictly necessary to execute core enterprise functions:

  • Photo Library & Document Storage: Used strictly when you select and attach supporting documents (e.g., medical certificates for sick leaves, receipt vouchers for reimbursement claims) via system Photo Pickers or Scoped Storage. The app does not access your personal photo library without your explicit action.
  • Biometric Authentication (Touch ID / Face ID / Android BiometricPrompt): If enabled on your iOS or Android device, biometric validation is processed locally by Apple's Secure Enclave or the Android Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). Biometric templates never leave your device and are never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on GCC-STAT servers.
  • Camera (Optional): Used solely if you choose to photograph a physical receipt or certificate directly within the app for attachment submission.
  • Location (Optional): If enabled for attendance verification, coarse location coordinates are validated strictly at the moment of attendance check-in to confirm authorized facility proximity. Location is not tracked continuously or in the background.

4. How We Use Your Information

All collected data is processed exclusively for institutional human resource operations:

  • Administering and logging employee leaves, permissions, and attendance.
  • Facilitating supervisor approval chains and institutional delegation.
  • Generating accurate payroll distributions and official salary statements.
  • Ensuring data integrity and compliance with GCC-STAT administrative regulations.

No Commercial Use: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information to third-party commercial entities, advertising networks, or data brokers.

5. Data Security and Storage

We implement rigorous institutional security protocols to protect employee data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or interception:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network transmissions between the mobile client and GCC-STAT servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Hardware Keystore Protection: Authentication tokens and sensitive local configurations are encrypted on-device using Apple Keychain (on iOS) and Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences (on Android).
  • Enterprise Backend Security: Enterprise transactional data is securely stored within dedicated, access-restricted SAP HANA data centers behind institutional firewalls.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics suites. Core system logs are stored strictly on GCC-STAT owned or managed private infrastructure.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Requests (Google Play & Apple Compliance)

Employment records, leave histories, and payroll statements are maintained in accordance with GCC-STAT institutional archiving policies and applicable statutory labor regulations.

Account Deletion & Data Erasure Procedure:
In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store user data policies:

  • Upon resignation, retirement, or termination of employment, employee access credentials to the mobile portal are automatically deactivated and revoked by the GCC-STAT HR Department.
  • To request the deletion of your mobile app session data, profile cache, or to initiate an institutional data review, you may submit a formal request directly to support@gccstat.org or via our Online Support Portal.
  • Upon verification of identity by the HR Department, local app access tokens and eligible mobile cached data will be purged. Statutory records (e.g. historical tax, pension, and payroll archives) are retained in compliance with applicable GCC legal retention obligations.
